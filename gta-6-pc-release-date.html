GTA 6 on PC: Is There a Release Date? What Rockstar Has Actually Said

Published Nov 16, 2026 · 1 min read · Leonida Wire

It is the question every PC player is asking, so here is the honest answer first: Rockstar has not announced a PC release date for GTA 6. As of November 10, 2026, there is no official PC version, no system requirements, and no PC pre-load. Anyone selling you a "GTA 6 PC key" today is selling something that does not exist.

What is confirmed

  • GTA 6 launches November 19, 2026 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S only.
  • Rockstar's store and support pages list no PC version, date or requirements.
  • Take-Two's November 9 results repeated the console launch date and said nothing about PC timing.

Why 2027 is expected (but not promised)

Reporting and analyst expectations point to a PC release in 2027, following Rockstar's usual pattern. With GTA 5, the console versions launched in September 2013 and the PC version followed in April 2015 — PC players waited, but the PC edition did come, with upgrades the console versions did not have at the time.

Treat every specific "PC release date" you see on social media as a guess until Rockstar's own Newswire says otherwise.

Should you wait for PC or play on console?

If you already own a PS5 or Xbox Series X|S, the console version is the only confirmed way to play at launch — and the only way to play this year. If you are PC-only, the realistic expectation is a wait into 2027, and this page will be updated the moment Rockstar confirms anything.
